## Introduction

For many of the IDEAs Clinic challenges, you may have the option to develop or expand on a website, app, or dashboard for your subproblem solution. This page should help you develop a basic understanding of some elements of web development. You do not need an in-depth knowledge of web development or design to make a good website, just enough so you know what you can do and how to look online for the ways to do it.
